Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Are PrymCare Health products approved for sale in Ghana?+
Yes. The products we distribute are cleared by Ghana's Food and Drugs Authority, and carry additional recognitions including Kosher rating, Halaal certification and International Aloe Science Council approval.
Do your products treat or cure disease?+
No. Our natural products are formulated to support, promote and maintain good health. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Please speak with a qualified doctor about any specific medical condition before starting a new supplement.
Can I become a PrymCare Health distributor?+
Absolutely — we welcome new partners into our business opportunity programme, with training, mentorship and ongoing support as you build your own wellness business.
Do you deliver across Ghana?+
Yes, we deliver within Accra and nationwide across Ghana, with international ordering support available for select products.
How do I know which products are right for me?+
Message us with your goal — whether it's energy, weight management or skin health — and we'll recommend a simple starting combination based on your needs and budget.
