Heart & Circulation Wellness

Your heart and circulatory system work continuously, and everyday habits — including your diet — can play a part in supporting how they function. The Heart & Circulation Wellness Pack brings together a set of Forever nutritional products for your everyday routine.

Facts to Know

  • Blood pressure is the measurement of the force of blood pushing against the walls of your blood vessels.
  • High blood pressure may occur without noticeable symptoms.
  • High blood pressure can affect both men and women.
  • Regular blood-pressure checks can help you know your current status.
  • Lifestyle and other health conditions can influence blood pressure.

This information is educational and general in nature. It is not medical advice. Please speak with a healthcare professional about your individual blood pressure and health.

Everyday Healthy Living

  • Eat a balanced diet.
  • Reduce excessive salt intake.
  • Exercise regularly.
  • Maintain a healthy body weight.
  • Avoid smoking.
  • Limit excessive alcohol consumption.
  • Manage stress.
  • Get adequate sleep.
  • Monitor your blood pressure.
  • Follow medical advice when necessary.
