Walk Out the Fear Today

High blood pressure can affect people without obvious warning signs. Understanding your blood pressure, paying attention to your lifestyle and getting appropriate professional advice can help you make better decisions about your health.

Many people live with concerns about high blood pressure for years without fully understanding the factors that may contribute to it.

Your body has natural systems that help regulate circulation and maintain normal body functions.

Supporting those systems through healthy food choices, physical activity, proper rest and appropriate healthcare is an important part of maintaining your wellbeing.

What Is High Blood Pressure?

Blood pressure is the measurement of the force of blood pushing against the walls of your blood vessels.

Your heart pumps blood through your arteries and blood vessels, carrying oxygen and nutrients to different parts of your body.

When blood pressure remains elevated over time, it can place additional strain on the heart and blood vessels. This is why regular monitoring and appropriate management are important.

Facts to Know

No obvious symptoms Affects everyone Check regularly
  • If blood becomes more difficult to circulate, the heart may have to work harder, which over time can make the heart lose a lot of energy.
  • Narrowed blood vessels can make it more difficult for blood to flow normally.
  • High blood pressure can affect both men and women.
  • High blood pressure may occur without noticeable symptoms.
  • Regular blood-pressure checks can help you know your current status.
  • Lifestyle and other health conditions can influence blood pressure.

What Are the Causes of This Now-Prevalent Condition?

While there may not always be one single cause of high blood pressure, several factors and health conditions can contribute to the problem. Some of the factors associated with high blood pressure include:

  • Free-radical and oxidative stress
  • Smoking
  • Overeating or excessive calorie intake
  • Lack of physical activity
  • Poor eating habits
  • Increasing age
  • Stress
  • High blood sugar or diabetes
  • Excessive alcohol consumption
  • Family history and genetics
  • Chronic kidney disease
  • Adrenal and thyroid disorders
  • Sleep apnea
  • Other underlying health conditions

Understanding these factors can help you take appropriate steps toward a healthier lifestyle.

Facts You May Need to Know

Blood pressure is normally recorded using two numbers:

  • Systolic blood pressure — the pressure when the heart contracts.
  • Diastolic blood pressure — the pressure when the heart relaxes between beats.

Blood-pressure categories can vary according to the medical guideline being used. Your healthcare professional can help you understand what your individual reading means.

Is There a Way to Keep Your Blood Pressure Normal for Life, Naturally?

The answer is: yes! To support healthy blood pressure, several areas of your lifestyle and overall health should be addressed.

  • Support your heart. A healthy lifestyle can help support normal heart function and overall cardiovascular health.
  • Support healthy blood vessels. Healthy eating, physical activity and other positive lifestyle habits can contribute to healthy blood vessels and circulation.
  • Pay attention to your diet. A balanced diet containing a variety of vegetables, fruits, whole grains, legumes, lean proteins and other nutritious foods can support general health.
  • Maintain healthy circulation. Regular physical activity and maintaining a healthy body weight can support cardiovascular health and normal circulation.
  • Monitor your blood pressure. Regular blood-pressure checks can help you understand your numbers and identify when professional medical advice may be necessary.
