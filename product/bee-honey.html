About Forever Bee Honey

Long before honey showed up on supplement shelves, it was simply how people in mountain communities sweetened their food. Forever Bee Honey keeps that tradition intact — no artificial additives, no heavy refining, just honey the way bees actually make it. In Ghana, it's become a go-to for people looking to cut back on processed sugar without giving up sweetness in their tea, porridge, or smoothies.

How to use it

There's no strict routine here — that's part of the appeal. A spoonful in warm water or tea in the morning is the most common way people take it, but it works just as well drizzled over fruit, stirred into oatmeal, or eaten straight off the spoon. One tablespoon (about 70 calories) is a typical single serving if you're watching your sugar intake.

Key benefits

  • Natural, fast-absorbing carbohydrates that can support daily energy levels
  • A gentler alternative to refined table sugar in drinks and meals
  • Fat-free, gluten-free, and free from artificial preservatives
  • Vegetarian-friendly, suitable for most everyday diets

Frequently asked questions

Is Forever Bee Honey raw or processed?

It's harvested and bottled with minimal processing to preserve its natural flavour and nutrients, unlike most heavily refined supermarket honey.

Can I use it as a sugar substitute?

Yes — many customers use it in tea, oatmeal, or smoothies in place of refined sugar, for natural sweetness plus trace minerals sugar doesn't offer.

Is it suitable for everyone?

It's gluten-free and vegetarian-friendly, but honey shouldn't be given to children under one year old. Check with a doctor if you have specific health conditions.

How fast is delivery in Accra?

We typically deliver within Accra within 24 hours of order confirmation. Other parts of Ghana may take a little longer depending on location.
